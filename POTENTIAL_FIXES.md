# Potential Fixes to Include

These are mods that would fit within the project guidelines of OpenFixes. However, not all of them have permissions compatible with the project. This list is meant to be a starting point on the authors to reach out to.

77 mods. Licenses and permissions checked on 2026-10-29 from each mod's source and Nexus page. Closed-source mods are not included in this list. All mods in this list are at least source-available and align with the project guidelines in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
